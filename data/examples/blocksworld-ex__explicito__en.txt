There are four blocks: A, B, C and D. A robot arm can pick up and hold one block at a time.

Initial state:
- Block D is on the table.
- Block A is on block D.
- Block B is on block C.
- Block C is on block A.
- Block B is clear (nothing is on top of it).
- The robot's hand is empty.

Goal:
- Block A must be on block B.
- Block C must be on block A.
- Block D must be on block C.

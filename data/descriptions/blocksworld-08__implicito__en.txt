There are nine blocks: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H and I. A robot arm can pick up and hold one block at a time. There is a stack made of, from bottom to top, A, H, G, I, C, D, B, E and F; A is on the table. The robot is not holding any block.

In the end the following must hold: D on I, I on A, A on B, B on H, H on G, G on F, F on E and E on C.

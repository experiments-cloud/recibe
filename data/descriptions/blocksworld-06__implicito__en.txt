There are six blocks: A, B, C, D, E and F. A robot arm can pick up and hold one block at a time. There is a stack made of, from bottom to top, B, E and F; B is on the table. There is a stack made of, from bottom to top, C, A and D; C is on the table. The robot is not holding any block.

In the end the following must hold: C on B, B on A, A on E, E on F and F on D.

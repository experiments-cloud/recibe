There are five blocks: A, B, C, D and E. A robot arm can pick up and hold one block at a time.

Initial state:
- Block B is on the table.
- Block A is on block B.
- Block C is on block A.
- Block D is on block E.
- Block E is on block C.
- Block D is clear (nothing is on top of it).
- The robot's hand is empty.

Goal:
- Block B must be on block E.
- Block C must be on block B.
- Block D must be on block C.
- Block E must be on block A.
